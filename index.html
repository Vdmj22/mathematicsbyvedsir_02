The Problem Most Schools Overlook

Applied Maths is NOT "Easy Maths"

Many schools club Applied Maths (241) with standard Core Maths (041), forcing Commerce & Humanities students to sit through irrelevant engineering trigonometry while completely neglecting:

  • Inferential Statistics & Probability: Poisson, Binomial, Normal Distribution, and hypothesis testing.
  • Marginal Cost & Revenue Calculus: Optimization for business economics rather than physics geometry.
Without dedicated faculty, students score poorly despite having sharp analytical aptitude.
